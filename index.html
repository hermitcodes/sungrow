A Kenyan agribusiness built around the farm.

Sungrow Agriculture Limited is an agricultural company led by Chief Executive Officer Munana Muthoni Njoroge. We work across the farming chain, from the field to the buyer, with a focus on dependable quality and fair dealing.

Whether you are a farmer looking for support, a buyer looking for a steady supplier, or a partner looking to work together, we would like to hear from you.

  • Rooted in the landPractices chosen to keep soil healthy for the next season.
  • Reliable from field to buyerClear communication and consistent quality.
  • Farmers firstSupport that fits real farms, not just plans on paper.
